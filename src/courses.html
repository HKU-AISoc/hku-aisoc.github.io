MATH2014

Sem 1 and 2

Difficulty: 2/7

Workload: 3/7

Seniors' Suggestions & Comments

  • Wolfram alpha will still be your friend
  • Think more on 3D space
  • Linear algebra are arrows in a high dimensional space
  • Do more questions

Why do I need to learn this

  • Basic multivariate calculus and linear algebra
  • multivariate calculus : Used in 2601, multivariate distribution (Bayesian statistics for AI)
  • Linear algebra : vectors and matrices used in neural networks and transformers
  • vectors in AI becomes "meaning" of information
